# Similar games

Checked October 14, 2026. These are comparisons of mechanics, not sales rankings.

| Game | Similarity | Difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Planarity](https://planarity.net/game.html) | Move graph vertices until edges no longer cross. The closest established core mechanic. | Our version adds authored short levels, anchors, geometric clearance rules, animation and an adaptive score. |
| [Simon Tatham's Untangle](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/puzzles/js/untangle.html) | The same graph-untangling family, with movable points connected by lines. | Our focus is a small tactile, audiovisual campaign. This does not make the underlying mechanic new. |
| [Tangle Master 3D](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rollic.tanglemaster3D) | Untangling and restoring visual order. | A three-dimensional rope-sorting game, rather than a planar graph puzzle. |

[Planarity's creator](https://johntantalo.com/wiki/Planarity/) dates the original to
2005. We should describe this experiment as our interpretation of untangling,
not a novel mechanic.
