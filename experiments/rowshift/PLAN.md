# Rowshift

## Pitch

Slide an entire row or column until four colored stones sit on their matching marks.
Every shift wraps around the board.

## M0

Find out whether moving several stones with one swipe creates useful, readable tradeoffs.
Play three short scrambles and decide whether the mechanic deserves more boards.

## Controls

Tap or click the arrows around the board to shift a row or column.
Z undoes the last move and R resets the board.

## Scope

This is a single player Godot 4.7 draft with a fixed goal and three reversible scrambles.
There are no generated levels, saves, purchases, ads, or store assets.

## Next decision

Watch whether players notice that every stone in the chosen line moves at once.
If they do, test a larger board with a move target before adding more content.
