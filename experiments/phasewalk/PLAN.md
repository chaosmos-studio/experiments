# Phasewalk

## Pitch

Cross a corridor by switching between two versions of the same place.
The rule is one sentence: move through open cells, then shift reality to pass walls.

## M0

Find out whether choosing *when* to shift feels more satisfying than simply walking around a wall.
Play three small boards with a shift budget, then decide whether to add more spatial structures or stop.

## Controls

Use arrow keys to move, Space or Shift to switch reality, and R to restart.
The on-screen controls also work by mouse or touch.

## Scope

This is a single player Godot 4.7 draft for desktop and touch play.
It has no campaign, save system, account, ads, or store assets.

## Next decision

Playtest whether players understand a wall that exists in one reality and disappears in the other.
If the boards feel like a straight corridor, try rooms with side paths before expanding content.
