# Undertow Bloom

Swim into a hostile deep sea, gather little lights, and lead them back to a coral reef.
The lights follow each other in a chain that hunters can break.
An expanding pulse pushes hunters away and needs time to recharge.

The playable slice tests escort, collection, group movement, predation and safe delivery.
A full game could explore migrating schools, giant sea creatures, changing currents, reef restoration, rescue routes and several distinct ocean zones.

## Controls

- Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to swim, Space to pulse.
- Phone: left stick to swim, right button to pulse.

Open `index.html` in a browser.
