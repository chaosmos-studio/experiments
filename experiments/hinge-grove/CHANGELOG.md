# Changes

## 0.2.0

- Tap and drag both select the seed that moves, with a live curved preview.
- Guided opening moves, larger board, readable coaching and larger touch targets.
- Roots spread, stems flex, leaves open and petals bounce into bloom with small leaf bursts.
- Reduced motion settles growth immediately and removes particles and sway.
- Save unfinished gardens and validated undo histories, including across level switching.
- Explain rooted, occupied and swept-corner moves; recover to before a blocked planting.
- Confirm restarts, show plant progress during play, and reserve route comparisons for completion.
- Completing garden 30 returns to unfinished gardens; the ending requires all thirty.

## 0.1.0

- Thirty gardens with paired seeds, rooting and blooms.
- Touch and keyboard controls, hints, undo and local progress.
- Original music, light UI and reduced motion.
