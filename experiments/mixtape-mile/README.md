# Mixtape Mile

Carry a cassette through a city at night.
Shift between three lanes, jump street obstacles and land tricks near a beat to charge the tape.
Deliver enough energy to each of three shows and a new musical layer joins the ride.

The playable slice tests a rhythm action loop with lane choice, timing, cassette condition, courier checkpoints and layered music.
A full game could connect neighborhoods, rival couriers, alternate routes, local musicians, evolving mixes and trick combinations.

## Controls

- Desktop: W/S or up/down to change lanes, Space to trick and jump.
- Phone: swipe vertically or use the left control to change lanes, right button to trick and jump.

Open `index.html` in a browser.
