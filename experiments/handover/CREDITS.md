# Credits

Handover is a Chaosmos game made with the help of AI tools.

## Art

The orbital greenhouse background and the three caretaker illustrations were generated for this game with AI tools.
The game interface and machine icons are drawn in Godot.

## Fonts

Outfit Light and Outfit Medium are by the Outfit Project Authors under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The license text is in `assets/fonts/Outfit-OFL.txt`.

## Audio

The three sound cues are synthesized by `tools/make_sfx.py` for this game.
