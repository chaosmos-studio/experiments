# Veylifter

Pilot a rescue skiff between a moving storm front and a carrier ship.
Hold steady over stranded beacons, carry two people at a time, and bank five rescues before the storm clock runs out.
Lightning forecasts its strike point before it lands.

The playable slice tests the movement, rescue hold, limited capacity, return journey, wind and timed danger that would anchor a larger aerial rescue game.
A full game could grow through changing weather cells, damaged islands, upgraded rescue craft, branching calls and choices about which distress signal to reach first.

## Controls

- Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to steer, Space to boost.
- Phone: left stick to steer, right button to boost.

Open `index.html` in a browser.
